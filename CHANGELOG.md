# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-09-24

Initial public archival release of the master's thesis computational package.

### Included

- CPLEX Branch-and-Cut baseline model.
- CPLEX hybrid-refinement model and working data file.
- Canonical HBIPSO-GR MATLAB implementation and MATLAB-to-CPLEX bridge utilities.
- 2030, 2035, and 2040 SCF Monte Carlo workbooks and maximum-demand workbook.
- Frozen MATLAB intermediate and result artifacts.
- With-EV and without-EV PowerWorld cases and display files.
- Optimization and PowerWorld comparison workbooks.
- Editable algorithm-flowchart source and retained reference visual.
- SHA-256 manifest, provenance map, and automated archival-integrity validation.

This release intentionally excludes degree-administration records, plagiarism reports, journal submission packages, stage reports, duplicate MATLAB source copies, and Monte Carlo PDF reports that duplicate released workbook content.


### Thesis metadata

- Official English thesis title: **Research on Electric Vehicle Charging Infrastructure Planning in Urban Areas**
- Institution: **Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology and Education (HCMUTE)**
